Mast: a client acquisition engine and workspace for freelancers that finds businesses in desperate need of their specific skills.

## The Problem
Freelancers struggle to find clients. Existing platforms and channels have high friction:
- Fiverr has high intensity competition.
- Upwork is too complicated for newer freelancers.
- Standard cold outreach suffers from dismal conversion: around 5 responses per 200 messages (3 automated, 2 saying they already have someone handling it) because outreach targets businesses that are already stable, popular, or already staffed for that role.

## The Solution: Mast
Mast is designed as a client-finding engine and complete workspace tailored for freelancers:
- **Targeted Lead Generation**: Rather than delivering generic business directories, Mast identifies businesses that actively need help in the freelancer's specific domain.
  - Example: A graphic designer searches for 10 coffee shops in the US with Instagram as the required contact channel. Mast's engine searches and filters for businesses with outdated logos, low-quality posts, or an apparent need for continuous creative ideas/design help.
- **Freelancer Workspace Suite**:
  - Full client acquisition pipeline.
  - AI-powered personalized message generation for outreach.
  - Follow-ups tracking tab.
  - "Focus" view that surfaces daily to-dos and priorities.
  - Gamification: Daily quests and milestone-based rewards for freelancers.

## The Founder
Remote freelancer with 2 years of experience who built Mast to solve their own client acquisition bottleneck.
