# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You couldn't answer how you'll maintain desktop Python scrapers when sites break or stop five freelancers from hitting the same local shop with cookie-cutter pitches, and without proof anyone will pay $79 a month instead of camping on your free tier, I have to ask if this is a business or a hobby—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, not a lead pool that lets five freelancers hit the same business in fifteen days with no answer on pitch differentiation, buyer intent, or API terms-of-service compliance — For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | When I ask myself, "Would I actually buy this?" at $79 a month for 400 manual leads I can't possibly send while four other freelancers pitch the same business every 15 days, the answer is no — For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | Your tier math still doesn't add up—you can't explain who burns 1,000 manual leads a day, whether 5 pitches every 15 days kills reply rates, or why anyone upgrades off a 300-lead free tier, so show me the numbers when you have them—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before—your Opportunity Score mistakes having a digital gap for willingness to pay to fix it, and with no cross-profession cap you'll flood the same local owners with pitch spam; For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Show me a resilient, zero-fire-drill update and anti-block architecture for the desktop worker alongside a real mechanism that keeps pitches distinct across the 5-user window and converts free users to paid tiers.
- **The Royalty Shark:** Show me written confirmation that your API providers permit Global Lead Pool caching and sharing, plus a mechanism that keeps pitches distinct across all five freelancers.
- **The Customer Shark:** Show me live reply-rate data proving the 2nd through 5th freelancer in the same profession still book meetings on the same business within 15 days, and give solo freelancers a paid-tier reason that isn't 400 leads they don't have time to manually send.
- **The Numbers Shark:** Live post-launch cohort math showing Free-to-Paid conversion rates, actual reply rates for the 1st through 5th freelancer pitching the same business in a 15-day window, and how many leads paid agencies actually contact per day.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Show me a verified buying-intent signal in your scoring that separates "ugly site" from "ready to buy," plus a hard cross-profession cooldown cap per business.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out how desktop scraper updates, selector fixes, and user IP blocks are managed at scale.
- The founder needs to find out how code detects an "outdated logo" and prevents 5 users sending similar hooks.
- The founder needs to find out how Mast keeps pitches distinct across those 5 users.
- The founder needs to find out what separates "has a gap" from "willing to pay to fix it."
- The founder needs to find out if the 6 API providers' TOS permit caching and sharing data in the Global Lead Pool.
- The founder needs to find out why solo users capped by manual outreach need 400 leads/day.
- The founder needs to find out how Mast protects reply rates for the 2nd–5th freelancer pitching the same business in 15 days.
- The founder needs to find out which paid tools users will cancel.
- The founder needs to find out who consumes 1,000 manual leads/day and how.
- The founder needs to find out whether 5 pitches per 15 days hurts reply rates.
- The founder needs to find out actual Free-to-Paid conversion rates post-launch.
- The founder needs to find out how Opportunity Score separates "has a gap" from "will pay to fix it."
- The founder needs to find out how to prevent cross-profession pitch overload.
- The founder needs to find out long-term reliability against residential IP blocks and site changes.
