# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You have zero data on your churn rate or customer acquisition cost while trying to sell software to broke freelancers; For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, but you came in here without a single paying customer, zero revenue, and no proof of cash flow to recoup my capital, and For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | When I ask myself "Would I actually buy this?", the answer is no, because you couldn't name a single tool in my current stack this replaces, and the minute I land client work I'll cancel your subscription to deliver the job, and For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | I always say "Show me the numbers," but you came in with zero revenue, unknown acquisition costs, and no proof that cash-strapped freelancers won't immediately churn; For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before: you're selling a subscription to the most churn-heavy demographic on earth with zero 90-day cohort retention data and zero empirical proof these cold pitches close paying clients; For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Three consecutive months of cohort data proving paying freelancers retain at under 5% monthly churn with a CAC under $30.
- **The Royalty Shark:** Bank-verified proof of at least $5,000 in monthly recurring revenue with stable month-over-month retention from paying freelance subscribers.
- **The Customer Shark:** Cohort data proving that paying solo freelancers stay subscribed for at least four consecutive months after closing a client rather than canceling during delivery.
- **The Numbers Shark:** Six consecutive months of cohort data showing at least 100 paying Pro subscribers with a blended CAC under $150 and monthly logo churn below 5%.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Verified 90-day cohort retention data proving paying freelancers retain at 75% or higher, backed by verified client contracts closed through the platform.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out the actual churn rate and customer acquisition cost (CAC).
- The founder needs to find out current paying subscriber count, monthly recurring revenue (MRR), and monthly churn rate.
- The founder needs to find out specifically which tools in a freelancer's current stack Mast replaces.
- The founder needs to find out current MRR, paying subscriber count, and blended CAC.
- The founder needs to find out the empirical free-to-paid conversion rate.
- The founder needs to find out the exact monthly logo churn rate.
- The founder needs to find out actual 90-day cohort retention rates.
- The founder needs to find out the exact rate-limiting and anti-blacklisting safeguards for desktop client IPs.
- The founder needs to find out empirical client close rates from live Mast outreach campaigns.
