# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: a client acquisition engine and workspace for freelancers that finds businesses in desperate need of their specific skills.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You couldn't tell me who is actually paying for this software or how you survive Meta banning your scrapers, leaving me asking is this a business or a hobby? For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, but with no pricing, zero paying subscribers, and not a dime in revenue, there is nothing to take a royalty on. For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | When I ask myself "Would I actually buy this?", the answer is no because broke freelancers won't pay a monthly subscription to cold-pitch businesses that have bad logos precisely because they have zero budget, and For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | Show me the numbers: with zero pricing data, unknown scraping costs, and not a single metric on churn, you have an expensive hobby rather than an investable business, and for that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before, and selling outreach tools to cash-strapped freelancers targeting businesses that don't care enough about design to pay for it guarantees fatal churn and banned accounts, and For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Twenty paying freelance subscribers who have renewed for at least three consecutive months and closed paying clients without triggering Meta account bans.
- **The Royalty Shark:** Showing at least 50 paying freelance subscribers generating steady monthly recurring revenue with positive gross margins after scrape and compute costs.
- **The Customer Shark:** Proof of 20 paying freelancer subscribers who closed paid client contracts from these leads and kept their subscription for three consecutive months.
- **The Numbers Shark:** Verified unit economics showing at least 50 paying subscribers with an 80% gross margin over API and scraping costs and a monthly churn rate under 5%.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Verified data showing at least 20 businesses flagged for poor branding actually hired freelancers and paid for redesign work, proving these leads convert and have real budget.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out pricing, monetization, and why beginner freelancers will buy software over free alternatives.
- The founder needs to find out how to prevent scraper and user account bans under Meta platform policies when scraping Instagram and sending cold DMs.
- The founder needs to find out product roadmap priorities and proof of a viable commercial business versus a personal tool.
- The founder needs to find out the monetization model, pricing tiers, and current paying customer count.
- The founder needs to find out customer acquisition cost (CAC), monthly churn rate, and how to retain freelancers after they land clients.
- The founder needs to find out server and compute costs for scraping and AI visual evaluation, along with gross margins per search.
- The founder needs to find out what existing tools freelancers will replace to buy Mast and what monthly price makes unit economics viable.
- The founder needs to find out empirical data proving businesses with poor branding have budget and convert.
- The founder needs to find out churn rates and retention strategy once freelancers reach client capacity.
- The founder needs to find out customer pricing, scraping and API compute costs, and gross margins.
- The founder needs to find out customer acquisition cost (CAC), lifetime value (LTV), and monthly churn rate.
- The founder needs to find out Mast's verified conversion rate and number of closed deals needed for freelancer ROI.
- The founder needs to find out evidence that businesses with poor branding actually have budget and convert from unsolicited DMs.
- The founder needs to find out technical protections to prevent Meta from banning user accounts when scraping and sending outreach.
- The founder needs to find out unit economics and pricing resilience given price-sensitive freelancers and high churn risks.
