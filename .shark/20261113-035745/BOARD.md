# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You dodged city lead exhaustion and home-IP scraper blocks while giving away 300 free leads a month, leaving me asking, "Is this a business or a hobby?" For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | You couldn't tell me who eats the cost of six API providers at 25,000 leads a month, how you stop one Premium agency from hoarding a city under your 15-day cooldown, or why a freelancer won't drop to Free the moment they land a gig—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | Asking myself "Would I actually buy this?" leaves me staring at a $29 Starter tier that hides the gaps I'm paying to find and a $79 Pro tier priced for 400 leads a day no solo freelancer can manually work: For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | Show me the numbers—25,000 AI drafts at $0.005 each already eat $125 of a $199 Premium plan before paying for six data APIs, and with zero math on CAC, payback, or city capacity under a 15-day cooldown; For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before, and selling up to 25,000 leads a month without proving lead quality holds up once top gaps are drained or that a local Python scraper survives anti-bot changes and ToS bans at scale is a fast track to a spam graveyard: For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Show me a live city cohort with paying conversions past the 300-lead free tier and hard data on how many users a city supports before the 15-day cooldown exhausts top leads.
- **The Royalty Shark:** I want a piece of every sale, so show me the exact API cost breakdown at 25,000 leads a month proving your 85% margin actually holds at full quota.
- **The Customer Shark:** Show me a solo-freelancer tier under $35/mo that includes website discovery and Opportunity Score ranking for 20-30 curated leads a day, plus real pilot data proving thriving local shops with bad sites actually buy despite a 15-day cooldown.
- **The Numbers Shark:** A unit-economics sheet proving >85% gross margin at maxed-out Pro and Premium quotas (including all six paid APIs and AI drafts) alongside modeled CAC, payback period, and per-city user capacity under the 15-day cooldown.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Show me retention and reply-rate data from a crowded city after months of heavy Pro/Premium usage proving lead quality doesn't collapse and the desktop worker doesn't break under provider blocks.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out how many users a city supports before top leads run out.
- The founder needs to find out how home-IP blocks and support are handled.
- The founder needs to find out who pays the 6 API providers and their cost at 25,000 leads/month.
- The founder needs to find out how to stop a Premium agency with unlimited seats from hoarding a city's leads via the 15-day cooldown.
- The founder needs to find out what stops freelancers from downgrading to Free mid-gig.
- The founder needs to find out how Starter users see gaps without website discovery or Opportunity Score ranking.
- The founder needs to find out if thriving shops with bad sites convert, and if 15-day re-pitching hurts responses.
- The founder needs to find out unit economics for maxed-out quotas including AI drafts and 6 API providers.
- The founder needs to find out expected CAC, Free-to-Paid conversion, and payback period.
- The founder needs to find out how many paying freelancers a city holds before starvation.
- The founder needs to find out how lead quality holds up once top gaps are exhausted.
- The founder needs to find out how the Python worker handles anti-bot changes, OS quirks, and ToS bans at scale.
