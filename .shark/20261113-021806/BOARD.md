# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You dodged who is actually going to pay, where your six paid API keys live in a desktop app, and why a shared lead pool won't just spam the same local shops, leaving me asking "Is this a business or a hobby?"—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, and right now you have no API cost math at 25,000 leads, no rule stopping ten local designers from spamming the same café, and no proof a booked-out freelancer won't cancel tomorrow — For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | When I ask myself, "Would I actually buy this?" I see 300 free leads that can already book me out, manual outreach I still have to send myself, and no answer on what tool I'd cancel or how you stop competing freelancers from flooding the same local business: For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | You claim over 85% gross margin, yet at full Premium usage your $0.005 AI drafts alone eat $125 of a $199 plan before 6 paid APIs, Free-tier costs, or any CAC and payback math exist—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before, where a shared lead pool turns into a spam herd chasing thriving offline businesses that never wanted a new website or logo in the first place: For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Show me a server-side proxy and cost cap for those six paid APIs, an anti-collision rule so the Global Lead Pool doesn't burn local leads, and a controlled test proving thriving offline shops with bad sites actually buy from cold outreach.
- **The Royalty Shark:** A verified per-lead API and AI cost breakdown at 25,000 monthly leads alongside a hard lead-cooldown rule and 90-day retention numbers from paying users.
- **The Customer Shark:** Show me a cohort of freelancers who stayed on the $79/mo Pro plan for 6+ months after landing clients, plus a concrete lead-exclusivity or cooldown rule and false-positive rate data on sparse local profiles.
- **The Numbers Shark:** A real unit-economics spreadsheet showing per-user AI and API costs at full Pro/Premium quotas, Free-to-paid conversion, target CAC, and payback months on the $29 Starter tier.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Live conversion data from at least 100 outreach attempts showing that high-Health, low-digital-score local businesses actually reply and buy, paired with a hard cooldown or exclusivity cap on Global Lead Pool leads.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out which segment actually converts and retains post-launch.
- The founder needs to find out where the 6 paid API keys are stored/proxied in the desktop worker and who absorbs API costs when Free users run 300 searches/month.
- The founder needs to find out whether thriving offline businesses actually buy digital services.
- The founder needs to find out exact API + AI costs at 25,000 Premium leads/month to prove >85% margin.
- The founder needs to find out how to prevent multiple local designers from pitching the same business.
- The founder needs to find out whether users actually stay subscribed once booked out.
- The founder needs to find out which existing tool/CRM users cancel to adopt Mast.
- The founder needs to find out how to prevent lead-pool overlap and identical AI pitches across competing users.
- The founder needs to find out how scoring avoids false positives on sparse profiles.
- The founder needs to find out exact margins at 6,000–25,000 AI drafts plus 6 paid APIs and Free-tier subsidies.
- The founder needs to find out target CAC, free-to-paid conversion rate, and payback months on Starter.
- The founder needs to find out how fast the Global Lead Pool saturates local markets and affects reply rates.
- The founder needs to find out via live tests whether offline-thriving businesses with bad sites buy.
- The founder needs to find out what cooldown or exclusivity stops 50 users from hitting the top-ranked lead.
- The founder needs to find out exact branding scoring criteria and false-positive rates.
