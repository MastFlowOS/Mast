### 3.4 Safest Correction for Phase 1
To achieve 100% score parity and safely eliminate the redundant `service.py score` subprocess in Phase 1:
1. **Pass full business context into `runEngineEnrich()`**: In `businessProcessingJob.ts`, pass `{ id, name, category, website, phone, instagram, facebook, linkedin, rating: reviews_rating, review_count: reviews_count, has_photos, is_disqualified, seo, blog, signals }` into `runEngineEnrich()`.
2. **Use a single canonical payload builder in Python**: Factor a shared helper `build_canonical_scoring_payload(candidate, website_intel, contact_intel, instagram_intel)` used by `ScoringWorker.process()` so it populates:
   - `instagram`: resolved across `candidate.instagram_url`, `instagram_intel.profile_url`, and `_website_instagram_url(contact_intel)`
   - `has_photos`: preserved from `candidate.has_photos`
   - `load_time_ms`: `round(website_intel.response_time * 1000)` when `website_intel.response_time is not None`
   - `website_is_weak`: `_is_weak_site(website)` (and persist `website_is_weak` to `businesses` so `score_business_v2` also sees `True`/`False` rather than `None`, or evaluate `ssl_valid` and `load_time_ms` in `compute_website_opportunity` whenever `website_is_weak is not True`)
3. **Normalize `profession_scores` format in `_enriched_to_dict()` (or accept both in `storeOpportunityScores.ts`)**: Emit `health_score`, `universal_breakdown`, and `profession_scores` keyed by `profession_slug` with `{ score, breakdown, summary, reasons }` so `computeAndStoreOpportunityScores()` and `computeAndStoreBusinessHealth()` can persist the precomputed result directly without spawning `service.py score`.
