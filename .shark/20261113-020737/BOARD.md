# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You built a complex desktop scraper with zero paying users, zero closed deals, and no answer on who actually pays or how you survive map API breakages, which begs the question "Is this a business or a hobby?"—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, and right now you have zero sales, zero breakeven math, and no proof freelancers won't burn your shared lead pool and cancel after one gig—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | Would I actually buy this when you haven't proved a single local owner pays from cold outreach, can't name one subscription I'd cancel, and won't stop other Mast users from pitching the exact same leads—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | You have zero live users, no CAC or payback math, and no full cost breakdown to back up your >85% gross margin claim on a $79/month Pro tier with 6,000 leads; For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before—zero closed deals, unproven cold DMs, and a shared lead pool with no city-level throttling that lets freelancers burn the same local businesses—For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Show me 20 paying freelancers on Starter or Pro who closed a real local business deal from Mast leads, plus a concrete fallback plan if Google, Apple, or Yelp cut off your data sources.
- **The Royalty Shark:** Live paying subscribers in your $29 to $199 tiers who closed real client deals and stayed subscribed for at least three months, plus the exact subscriber count needed to break even.
- **The Customer Shark:** Show me 20 paying freelancers who replaced another paid tool with Mast, closed $500+ local-business deals from uncontested leads, and stayed subscribed for three months after filling their pipeline.
- **The Numbers Shark:** Show me the numbers—a full Pro-tier unit cost breakdown at 6,000 leads/month alongside live pilot data proving actual reply rates, CAC, and post-gig churn.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Live conversion data from at least 50 paying freelancers showing real closed deals from cold outreach and multi-month retention without lead-pool collisions in the same city.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out via live paid conversions.
- The founder needs to find out by running real outreach to local owners.
- The founder needs to find out a resilient fallback for Google/Apple/Yelp sources.
- The founder needs to find out overhead and subscriber count needed for real cash.
- The founder needs to find out how to stop local users from burning the same leads.
- The founder needs to find out real post-gig churn.
- The founder needs to find out how many users can pitch one business simultaneously.
- The founder needs to find out which paid spend users cut and how to prevent pauses when full.
- The founder needs to find out exact unit economics at full usage.
- The founder needs to find out estimated CAC, payback period, and monthly churn.
- The founder needs to find out real reply rates and retention if replies stay at 2.5%.
- The founder needs to find out via live tests if thriving local businesses buy from cold DMs.
- The founder needs to find out how to prevent pool lead collisions in one city.
- The founder needs to find out real Free-to-paid conversion and post-gig churn.
