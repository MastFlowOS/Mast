# No deal: all 5 sharks are out

**The pitch:** Mast: an intelligent client acquisition engine and CRM workspace for freelancers that identifies thriving local businesses with critical digital gaps and converts them through research-backed outreach.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tech Billionaire Shark | OUT | none | You couldn't tell me who's actually paying, why freelancers won't cancel after landing one gig, or what happens when Google and Yelp block your scrapers—leaving me asking, "Is this a business or a hobby?"—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Royalty Shark | OUT | none | I want a piece of every sale, and right now you have zero paying users, unpriced map API costs at 25,000 leads a month, and zero proof anyone upgrades past your 300-lead free tier—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | When I ask myself, "Would I actually buy this?" the answer is no—your Free tier already gives me 300 leads a month, and you have zero proof freelancers will pay $79/mo, stick with manual outreach past week one, or stay subscribed once they land two clients, so for that reason, I'm out. |
| The Numbers Shark | OUT | none | Show me the numbers, because right now your >85% margin claim has zero API cost per lead, zero CAC, zero churn math, and zero conversion proof—For that reason, I'm out. |
| The Skeptic Shark | OUT | none | I've seen this movie before—a shared Global Lead Pool turns the best local businesses into a spam graveyard while your desktop scrapers sit one IP ban away from breaking, and you have zero live proof anyone actually buys from these DMs. For that reason, I'm out. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Tech Billionaire Shark:** Show me 50 freelancers paying $79/month for Pro who stay subscribed for at least three consecutive months after landing their first client.
- **The Royalty Shark:** Show me 50 paying subscribers staying past month two alongside real map API bills that prove your 85% gross margin holds.
- **The Customer Shark:** Live cohort data from your first 100 Free-tier freelancers showing how many upgrade to $29 or $79/mo, survive week-one manual outreach, and stay subscribed for three months after landing a client.
- **The Numbers Shark:** Live data from real users showing exact map-API cost per enriched lead, Free-tier burn per converted customer, 90-day churn, and Mast's actual reply-to-close rate.
- **The Skeptic Shark:** Show me a 30-day live cohort of paying freelancers proving that pool-deduplicated leads beat your 2.5% reply baseline and close real local clients without scraper blocks.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out which segment actually pays and why it hasn't launched to paying users yet.
- The founder needs to find out whether those prevent cancellation once a freelancer has steady clients.
- The founder needs to find out the fallback plan and margin impact if sources block or rate-limit scrapers.
- The founder needs to find out if these gates convert Free users.
- The founder needs to find out exact map/directory API costs at 25,000 leads/month.
- The founder needs to find out with live data whether freelancers pay monthly after landing a client.
- The founder needs to find out if solo freelancers will pay $79/mo over Free.
- The founder needs to find out real week-one drop-off rates.
- The founder needs to find out if users stay subscribed with a full calendar.
- The founder needs to find out exact API cost per enriched lead and Free-tier burn per converted user.
- The founder needs to find out CAC, payback period, and actual churn/LTV.
- The founder needs to find out Mast's real reply-to-close rate.
- The founder needs to find out through live tests whether Mast drafts beat 2.5% and local shops buy from cold DMs.
- The founder needs to find out how to stop multiple freelancers from pitching the same top-scoring leads in the Global Lead Pool.
- The founder needs to find out how the desktop worker survives IP bans and scraper breakage on Google Maps and Yelp.
